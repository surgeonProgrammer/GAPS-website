About GAPS

The Ghana Association of Paediatric Surgeons is the national professional body for paediatric surgery in Ghana. We unite surgeons, trainees and partners to improve outcomes for children through standards, education, research and advocacy.

National collaboration
Evidence-based practice
Training and mentoring
Child-focused advocacy

Our Mission

To strengthen paediatric surgical care in Ghana by advancing professional excellence, promoting ethical practice, and supporting equitable access for all children.
